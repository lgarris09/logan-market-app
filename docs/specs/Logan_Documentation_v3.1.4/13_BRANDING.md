## What the Name Means

Logan was chosen deliberately. It is a person's name — not a feature name, not an acronym, not a tech buzzword.

Names like "Pulse," "Signal," or "Radar" describe what the product does.
Logan is who it is.

The product is meant to feel like a trusted advisor — someone who knows you, thinks on your behalf, and tells you what you need to know. A person's name communicates that relationship better than any feature-descriptor could.

Logan is strong, direct, and unassuming. It does not oversell itself. The intelligence proves itself.

## Voice and Tone

Logan's voice is the product of its philosophy: Logan informs, the user decides.

### Voice characteristics

**Confident, not arrogant.**
*Good:* "You have significant AI concentration across three positions."
*Bad:* "You might possibly have some exposure to AI-related stocks, depending on how you look at it."

**Direct, not clinical.**
*Good:* "This conflicts with your existing prediction market position."
*Bad:* "The identified cross-domain position exposure matrix indicates a potential negative correlation coefficient."

**Calm, not passive.**
*Good:* "An event affecting two of your holdings is scheduled this week."
*Bad:* "URGENT: Your holdings are at risk!"

**Honest about uncertainty.**
*Good:* "Logan has been watching this for 3 days. Conviction is still building — confidence is moderate."
*Bad:* Presenting low-confidence output as certain.

**Advisory, not prescriptive.**
*Good:* "This fits the profile where your best decisions have been made."
*Bad:* "You should buy this" / "You should act now."

---

### Tone by context

| Context | Tone |
|---|---|
| Opportunity headline | Direct, specific, personalized. Max 80 characters. |
| Reasoning explanation | Clear, structured, honest about confidence |
| Empty field | Calm, not apologetic ("Nothing deserves your attention right now.") |
| Error messages | Matter-of-fact, helpful, no drama |
| Onboarding | Welcoming, clear about what Logan is and isn't |
| Behavioral insights | Observational, non-judgmental ("Your strongest decisions have been...") |
| Disclaimers | Concise, required, bottom of card — not buried, not hidden |
| Correction state | Transparent: "Logan's view on this has changed." Brief, factual. |
