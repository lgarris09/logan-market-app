## Intelligence Features

**Counterfactual Engine**
"What would have happened if you had acted on this when Logan first surfaced it?"
Compares the user's actual behavior against Logan's recommendations to show the value of following (or not following) Logan's advice. Powerful for trust-building. Extension point already reserved in V1 architecture.
*Tagged: V2, intelligence, learning*

**Accumulation Pattern Detector**
Detect slow, quiet accumulation of a position across multiple small transactions — often a signal before a large move. Currently deferred from V1 hit detection.
*Tagged: V2, detection*

**Quiet-Before-Move Detector**
Detect unusually low volatility or volume before a historically volatile event. Often a leading indicator.
*Tagged: V2, detection*

**Historical Analog Matching**
"Logan has seen a pattern like this 12 times in the last 5 years. Here's what happened 8 of those times."
Pattern recognition at the market/sports level — not just this user's history, but historical precedent.
*Tagged: V2, intelligence*

**Cross-User Intelligence (Aggregate, Anonymized)**
What patterns are performing well across all Logan users? Which detector types are most accurate in which domains? Never user-identifying — pure aggregate signal.
*Tagged: V3, intelligence, privacy-sensitive*

**Sentiment Timing Intelligence**
Not just what sentiment is, but where it is in its cycle. Peak sentiment often precedes reversals. Logan could detect sentiment maturity.
*Tagged: V2, detection*

**ML-Based Trigger Code Discovery**
Instead of a manual registry, use machine learning to identify recurring signal patterns that predict outcomes and automatically propose new trigger codes. V1 uses manual registry only. V2 extension point.
*Tagged: V2, TriggerEvent, learning*

## Product Features

**Projects Module**
Users create Projects — Career, Real Estate, Business, Family, Health. Each project has persistent memory, documents, notes, timelines, and AI analysis. Logan reasons about cross-project connections.
Example: "Your job search timing might affect your ability to qualify for a mortgage — these two projects are connected."
*Tagged: V3, product expansion*

**Intelligence Feed**
A browsable context view — not the urgency of the Opportunity Field, but a softer "what's happening that's relevant to me." For users who want to explore, not just respond to alerts.
*Tagged: V2, UI*

**Expert Workspace**
Structured analysis templates the user fills out. Logan learns from how they think through problems. Over time, Logan applies the user's own reasoning framework to new situations.
*Tagged: V3, learning*

**Chat with Logan's Reasoning**
Direct conversation with the intelligence layer. "Why did you surface this?" "What would change your mind about this?" "What are you watching on X right now?"
*Tagged: V2/V3, product*

**"Logan has been watching this" Historical View**
Show the user a timeline of how Logan's conviction on something built up over days or weeks before it surfaced. Makes the intelligence feel earned, not sudden.
*Tagged: V2, transparency*

**Portfolio Scenario Testing**
"What would happen to your portfolio if X occurred?" Run scenarios against current positions. Not a prediction — a stress test.
*Tagged: V3, product*

**Trigger Code Performance Dashboard**
Let users see which trigger codes Logan has historically found most predictive in their domains. Builds trust and helps users calibrate how much weight to put on different signal types.
*Tagged: V2, transparency, TriggerEvent*

## UI / Experience

**3D Orbital Opportunity Field**
The field tilted slightly, nodes orbiting the central core at different radii and elevations. High-conviction items orbit close and bright. Distant items orbit at the outer rings. Full 3D with depth, rotation, perspective. V1 uses 2D with depth illusion.
*Tagged: V2, UI, Skia*

**Haptic Intelligence**
Subtle haptic patterns that communicate confidence or urgency without requiring the user to look at the screen. Different haptics for Action Window vs. routine surface.
*Tagged: V2, UX*

**Ambient Home Screen Widget**
iOS/Android widget showing the current field state — a small version of the Opportunity Field on the home screen. Glanceable intelligence without opening the app.
*Tagged: V2, mobile*

**Watch Integration**
Apple Watch / Wear OS complication showing Logan's current top opportunity. One-tap to open full detail on phone.
*Tagged: V3, hardware*

## Business / Platform

**Enterprise Version**
Company-licensed Logan. Teams share an intelligence layer. Shared hypotheses, team memory, company-level opportunity tracking. Sales intelligence, competitive intelligence, market timing.
*Tagged: V3, business*

**API for Integrations**
"Powered by LI" — Logan's intelligence as an API. Third-party apps can query Logan's reasoning for entities in their domain.
*Tagged: V3, platform*

**Logan for Financial Advisors**
Advisor-facing version. Logan analyzes a client's portfolio and surfaces intelligence the advisor can use in client conversations. Not replacing the advisor — making them faster and smarter.
*Tagged: V3, business*

## Hardware (Long-Term Vision)

**Ambient Intelligence Device**
A small, always-on device that sits on your desk. No screen necessary — ambient light patterns communicate Logan's current confidence level. A soft glow that intensifies when something deserves attention. Silent, present, intelligent.
*Tagged: long-term, hardware*

**Audio Intelligence**
Brief audio briefings: "Logan's morning brief" — 60 seconds of what deserves your attention today, spoken naturally. Not a robotic readout — written by Logan, delivered as audio.
*Tagged: V3, audio*

## Technical Ideas

**ML-Based Decay Rate Calibration**
Instead of fixed decay rates, learn per-user optimal decay from outcome data. Users who act quickly may benefit from faster decay (prevents stale recommendations). Users who act slowly benefit from slower decay.
*Tagged: V2, learning*

**Automated Outcome Detection**
Instead of relying on manual outcome recording, automatically detect when an opportunity's underlying event has resolved. Price targets hit, game results, contract resolution. Currently deferred to V2.
*Tagged: V2, learning*

**Memory Portability**
Users can export and import their Logan memory. Useful for account migration or backup. Also enables: "let me show you what Logan knows about me."
*Tagged: V3, privacy, product*
