## Who this beta is for

People who already keep an eye on at least a few stocks or company events, and who would like to spend
less time checking.

## What STRATUS is

STRATUS watches for meaningful changes in the companies you care about and tells you what changed, why
it may matter to you, and how well-supported the observation is. The aim is that you notice what matters
without monitoring constantly.

## What STRATUS is not

- Not financial advice. STRATUS does not tell you what to buy, sell or hold.
- Not a stock recommendation engine.
- Not guaranteed real-time coverage. It can be late, and it can miss things.
- Not an institutional research terminal.

When STRATUS describes evidence as stronger or weaker, it is describing how well-supported the
observation is. It is not a prediction and not a probability that a price will move.

Sometimes STRATUS will say less than you expect. If it cannot stand behind a comparison or cannot confirm
how current something is, it leaves it out or says so.

## What to do

Use it the way you naturally would.

- **Watch** the companies you actually want monitored.
- **Open** the opportunities that matter to you.
- **Ask STRATUS** from an opportunity when the context is unclear.
- Tap **Something off?** on an opportunity when it is:
  - wrong,
  - stale,
  - not useful to you,
  - confusing about why you got it,
  - or not what you expected.

A report takes a few seconds and is tied to that exact opportunity, so we can see what you saw.

## What we want to learn

- Does STRATUS save you monitoring effort?
- Do opportunities reach you while they still matter?
- Are the explanations useful?
- Are the interruptions earned?
- Do you trust what STRATUS says, and what it declines to say?

We will ask to talk with you briefly during the beta. Specific examples help most: "this one I would have
missed", "this one I did not understand".

## What we collect

What you open, what you Watch, the questions you ask STRATUS, and the reports you send, each tied to the
opportunity it concerns. This is used to judge whether STRATUS is truthful, timely, understandable and
useful. Deleting your account deletes it.
